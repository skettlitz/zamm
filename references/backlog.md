# Backlog — index

The backlog holds LATENT intentions — what might be done — as immutable
records under `zamm-memory/backlog/<YYYY>/`, compiled into a pulled lens
(`backlog list`), never into the session-start budget. It exists so an idea
is neither lost in conversation nor parked as a Draft plan: a plan is
committed work with obligations, an idea has none.

Boundary test, which is the whole decision:

- implies future action, no commitment → backlog (here)
- asserts a durable reusable claim → knowledge
- commits to doing something → plan
- happened, noteworthy, and neither → journal

Then load only what you are about to do:

| you are about to | read |
| --- | --- |
| look at what is pending, or filter by topic | `backlog-reading.md` |
| capture, sharpen or vote on an idea | `backlog-writing.md` |
| mark or unmark, promote into a plan, retire, or reason about decay | `backlog-maintenance.md` |

Reading is free and an idea is one sentence. Marking pushes an idea into
every session's digest, and promoting creates a plan with obligations —
`backlog-maintenance.md` before either.
